# Administrator guide

For Msendo Noble Homes staff. No technical knowledge needed.

## Signing in

1. Go to `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/admin` and sign in with your work email and password.
2. If you set up two-step verification, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. Forgot your password? Press **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.

Turn on two-step verification under **My account**. It is strongly recommended for everyone and expected for administrators.

What you see in the menu depends on your role. If a page says your role does not include access, ask a super administrator.

## Adding a property

1. **Properties > Add property**.
2. Fill in **Basics**: a clear title (e.g. "4-bedroom terrace duplex with BQ, Jabi"), whether it is for sale, rent or short-let, and the property type. Leave the web address blank to create it automatically.
3. **Price** or **Rent**: enter numbers only (commas are fine). For rentals choose how often rent is paid.
4. **Additional charges**: add the caution deposit, agency fee, legal fee and service charge as separate lines. Tick **Percentage** for fees such as "10% of rent". Never add these to the rent.
5. **Location**: choose the district. The street address stays private unless you tick **Show street address publicly**. Only tick **Show map pin publicly** if the owner has agreed.
6. **Specifications** and **Amenities**: fill in what applies.
7. **Title and documents**: write the document type. Only a publisher may tick **verified**, and only after the documents have been checked.
8. Press **Create listing**. The listing is saved as a draft with a reference such as `MNH-S-01007`.

## Photos and videos

On the property's page, scroll to **Photos and videos**.

1. Drag files onto the box, or press **Browse files** (works on phones and tablets too).
2. Check the previews. Add **alt text** to each photo describing what it shows (e.g. "Living room with garden view"); this helps blind visitors and search engines.
3. Press **Upload**. Progress bars show each file. If your connection drops, press **Retry**; uploads resume where they stopped.
4. After uploading:
   - Drag cards, or use the arrow buttons, to change the order.
   - **Main photo** sets the cover image used on listing cards.
   - **Edit details** changes captions, alt text, video titles and descriptions, or hides an item from the public site.
   - Videos get a poster image automatically. To change it, use **Edit details > Replace poster image**.
   - **Remove** takes an item off this listing. Users with delete rights are asked whether to delete the file permanently.
5. To reuse a video already uploaded for another listing, choose it under **Add a video already uploaded for another listing**.

Accepted: JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF photos; MP4 video (export from your phone or editor as MP4/H.264). Size and length limits are in **Site settings > Upload limits**. Location data stored in phone photos is removed automatically.

## Publishing and status

Use the **Status** box on the property page:

| Button | Effect on the website |
| --- | --- |
| Publish | Appears in search, sale/rent pages and galleries |
| Mark reserved | Still listed, with a "Reserved" badge |
| Mark sold / Mark let | Removed from search; the page stays online with a "no longer available" message if that option is ticked |
| Mark unavailable | Same as sold/let, for withdrawn properties |
| Unpublish | Back to draft, hidden from the public |
| Archive | Hidden from the public, kept for records |
| Delete | Permanent. Use Archive instead unless the listing was created by mistake |

Listing editors cannot publish. They press **Submit for review**, and a property manager publishes it.

**Duplicate as draft** copies a listing (without photos) so you can add a similar unit quickly.

**Preview** shows a draft exactly as visitors will see it.

## Enquiries

**Enquiries** lists every form submission: property enquiries, contact messages, listing submissions and data requests. New ones are highlighted.

Open an enquiry to:

- Call, WhatsApp or email the person with one press.
- Change the **status** (New, Contacted, Inspection scheduled, In progress, Converted, Closed, Spam).
- **Assign** it to a colleague and set a **follow-up date**. Overdue follow-ups show in red.
- Add **internal notes**. Customers never see notes.
- **Delete** it when the person asks for their data to be erased, or for spam.

## Inspections

Online bookings arrive as **requests**. They are not confirmed until you confirm them.

1. **Inspections > Needs action**, open the request.
2. Call the customer, agree a time, choose the **agent**, then press **Confirm inspection**. The system will not let you book an agent for two overlapping inspections.
3. The customer receives a confirmation email if they gave an address, and both the customer and the agent get a reminder the day before.
4. Afterwards, mark it **completed** or **no-show**. Use **Reschedule** or **Cancel** if plans change. Every change is kept in the history.

## Blog

1. **Blog > New article**. Write a title, a short summary, and the article. Use the toolbar for headings, lists, links and images.
2. Choose a category and add tags.
3. Upload a featured image and describe it.
4. Status: **Draft** to keep working, **Scheduled** to publish later at a set time, **Published** to go live now, **Archived** to take it down.
5. **Preview** shows the article before it goes live.

## Site settings (administrators)

- **Company, Contact, Social media, Logo**: changes appear across the site and in emails within seconds.
- **Search engines**: default title and description, sharing image, Google verification code.
- **Analytics**: paste your Google Analytics ID. Visitors are asked for consent first.
- **Inspection booking rules**: opening hours, notice period, Sunday bookings.
- **Notifications**: who receives new enquiry alerts.
- **Testimonials**: add only real quotes from clients who have given written permission, and tick the permission box.

## Categories and locations

Add new property types, Abuja districts or new cities under **Categories and locations**. Untick **Active** rather than deleting anything listings use. Tick **Featured on home page** for districts to show on the home page.

## Staff (super administrators)

**Staff and roles > Invite a staff member**: enter their name, work email and role. They receive an email to set a password. If the email cannot be sent, a one-time link appears for you to share securely. Change a person's role or untick **Active** to remove access instantly. Give each person the least access they need.

## Audit log

Every change to listings, media, enquiries, inspections, articles, staff and settings is recorded with who made it and what changed.
